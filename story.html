THE PERSON BEHIND THE SCREEN

More than a name.

YASHH / 001

Hey, I'm Yashh.

I’m in 12th, preparing for JEE, I write when I can’t explain what I feel, poetry, stories aur woh saari baatein jo shayad kisi ko keh nahi pata ✍️🌙.

Some of my best memories are with people I will never talk to again. But I'll always cherish those moments.

I'm scared of attachments because I believe every attachment that I have will finally break my heart or leave me. The only time I cry is when I lose the people that I love.

Wanting to be an actor, a filmmaker, write books, be a musician, a poet, a therapist, I wanna heal people, be a teacher in kids' classes, a tech guru, someone's mum, a director, a model, a Realtor, a wife, an editor, and also want to be a farmer, who grows only beautiful flowers.

How I love flowers! And yes, I also love to disappear.

And there have been days I wonder if all these are not too much for just me? But the funny thing is, I have lived through all these days battling with this question, while becoming each and every thing on my list.

The only thing left for me to be on my list is a teacher — teaching children. I still look forward to doing that someday.

However, I see so many new things crawling into my list to make up a new list for me. And it's a beautiful thing journeying through time, and becoming each and every thing.

A collage of everything I ever wanted to be 🤌❤️.

You become that you hate 💗🤞.

“

Somewhere between the life I’m living and the life I dream about, there’s a version of me waiting to be discovered.
